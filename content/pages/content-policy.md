## What this site is

This site collects **color-coded lyrics** (each line colored by the member who sings it), **romanization**, **translations**, **credits** and **line distribution** statistics per song.

## Lyrics and copyright

- Lyrics displayed on this site are the **exclusive property of their authors and rights holders**.
- We only publish lyrics for which we believe a legitimate basis exists (official release, fan translation of an officially released song, or the rights holder's explicit permission).
- **All sample content in this repository is original placeholder text** written for development. It must be replaced with properly licensed content before the site goes live.
- We do not use machine translation as a primary source: translations are made by people and are credited to their translators.

## Color coding

- The default basis for color coding is the **studio audio** of the release.
- Live, remix or alternate versions are coded **only when explicitly noted**, in a dedicated version with its own line coding.
- Member colors aim to follow official or widely accepted fandom conventions. When a color is a placeholder, `colorSource` says so.
- Line attribution uses **internal member IDs** (`memberIds`), never display names, so renames never break data.

## What is not allowed

- Uploading unreleased material, leaks or anything that may infringe on an artist's rights.
- Impersonating artists, labels or official channels.
- Spam, offensive content, or link-farming in comments.
- Submitting machine-generated translations or corrections without a human review.

## Takedowns and corrections

Rights holders or concerned parties can report any content:

1. Use the **Report a correction** form on the song page, or
2. Open an issue in the [content repository](https://github.com/YOUR_GITHUB_USERNAME/YOUR_REPO).

Every report becomes a **GitHub issue** that editors review. Corrections are never applied automatically.
