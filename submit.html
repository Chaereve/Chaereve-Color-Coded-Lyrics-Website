Submit lyrics

Got lyrics, a romanization or a translation? Send them in — you keep full credit.

Guidelines

  1. Credit is sacred. Tell us who wrote the lyrics, romanization and translation — every post is credited to its authors.
  2. One line per singer. Split lines the way they are sung, and mark who sings each line (or “All”).
  3. Keep the columns apart. Send original, romanization and translation as separate blocks — our editors line them up.
  4. Link your sources. If the original lyrics come from an album booklet or a label site, include the link.
  5. Personal use only. By submitting, you agree the work can be published here with credit and shared for personal use.
