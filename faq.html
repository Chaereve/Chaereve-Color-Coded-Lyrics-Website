FAQ

Everything about color coding, credits and how this site works.

What is color-coded lyrics?

Every line of the song is written in the color of the member singing it. The legend at the top of each song tells you which color belongs to whom — lines for everyone are shown in white (or black on the light theme).

What do the lyric sections mean?

Each song is shown in three separate sections like Color Coded Lyrics: Romanization is the original lyrics written in Latin letters so you can follow the sound, Hangul (or Original) is the lyrics in their own script, and Translation is the English meaning. The order of the lines is the same in every section.

Can I use your lyrics or translations?

You are free to share them for personal use with credit to the listed translator and a link back to Chaereve. Please do not rehost or use them in monetized media without permission.

How do I request a song?

Head to the Requests page and send your request there — you can see what the team is already working on before adding yours.

Why is a song missing a section?

Some songs have not been translated or romanized yet, so that section is left out. If something looks wrong or you can help fill in the gap, tell us on the Requests page.

How often are new lyrics added?

Usually within a few days of release. Follow the RSS feed in the footer to catch every new post.

Who runs Chaereve?

Fans, for fans. Every post is credited to the people who wrote the lyrics, romanization and translation — we just line them up nicely.
